Travel

Fast Travel Guide

Big Walk travel becomes easier as trains, chairlifts and tunnels are unlocked; each system works best with a shared destination.

Quick answer: Big Walk travel becomes easier as trains, chairlifts and tunnels are unlocked; each system works best with a shared destination.

What to do

Check the map, choose a rendezvous point, then pick the transport route that gets the full group closest to it.

Helpful checks

Use transport to reduce repeat walking during cleanup, not to split the group before a cooperative puzzle.

Frequently asked questions

Is this official information?

Multiplayer, saves, joining, communication and troubleshooting facts are checked against the official Big Walk FAQ. Route notes are fan-made and should be rechecked after updates.

What if our group is still stuck?

Regroup at a known landmark, confirm the host and session state, then work through one check at a time rather than changing several things at once.
