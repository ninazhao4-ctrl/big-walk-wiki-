Big Walk Ending Guide

This page is for groups approaching the final route or choosing to prepare without reading a full story breakdown. Stop here if you want the remaining structure to stay completely unknown.

Quick answer: Before entering the final stretch, make sure the people who want to see the ending are present and the host is ready to continue the saved world.

Community gameplay screenshot supplied by the site owner. This independent guide is not affiliated with House House or Panic.

Before the finale

  • Bring the regular group together when possible.
  • Finish any immediate exploration you do not want to postpone.
  • Keep a note of unfinished puzzles for a later cleanup pass.

What to expect

  • Big Walk has a hand-crafted cooperative ending.
  • The main route is not the same as full completion.
  • Returning after the credits can be useful for optional activities and missed goals.

After the credits

  • Review achievements and collections separately.
  • Use the map and fast routes for efficient cleanup.
  • Only open detailed post-game puzzle guides when your group accepts spoilers.
