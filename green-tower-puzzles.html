Green Tower Puzzles & Chairlift Route

Green Tower progression opens the chairlift route. It is especially helpful for mountain travel and for revisiting high-ground puzzle areas.

Quick answer: Green Tower progression opens the chairlift route. It is especially helpful for mountain travel and for revisiting high-ground puzzle areas.

Before you begin

Bring a communication tool if players may separate vertically. Agree on a landmark and a meeting point before anyone rides ahead.

Route checklist

Collect rewards near the tower; complete the tower progression; follow the key route carefully; use the prepared key at the chairlift destination.

After the unlock

Record both ends of the chairlift; combine it with map landmarks; use it for high-ground exploration rather than guessing a walking route each time.
