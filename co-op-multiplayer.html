Big Walk Co-op & Multiplayer

Big Walk is an online cooperative adventure built for 2–12 players. The host keeps the group’s save, while friends join through platform connections or a Join Code.

Quick answer: Choose the host before you begin. The same person needs to open the saved world whenever your group wants to continue that journey.

Community gameplay screenshot supplied by the site owner. This independent guide is not affiliated with House House or Panic.

How sessions work

  • One player hosts the world and holds the automatic save.
  • Friends can join an active session through their platform friend list or a Join Code.
  • When the host ends the session, the rest of the group disconnects.

Choosing a world size

  • The host selects a 2-player, 3-player or 4+ world configuration.
  • Choose based on the smallest reliable group, not the largest number of visitors you expect.
  • That setting changes some challenge configurations but does not cap the number of people who can join.

Good group habits

  • Decide who hosts before a long session.
  • Use the game’s voice or text systems to preserve its communication challenges.
  • Keep screenshots or notes for unfinished puzzles, landmarks and coordinates.
