Big Walk Puzzles & Solutions

A route-first index for the Drawbridge and tower challenges. Read the hint, try it with your group, then reveal more only if you need it.

Quick route: Complete four challenges in the opening area to earn the key, pass it through the key cutters, cross the Drawbridge, then use the tower landmarks to organize the wider island.

Starting area & Drawbridge

The opening route teaches the game’s central language: one player can see information another player needs. Keep verbal directions simple, describe colors and shapes before acting, and bring successful pieces back toward the central bridge.

Known opening challenges

  • Blue Room
  • Green Structure
  • Mechanical Arm
  • Cave challenge

Red Tower route

Use the Red Tower as your first major navigation anchor beyond the bridge. Community names for challenges can vary, but this route commonly includes a green room, yellow maze, train-station cannon, basketball court and mountain gesture challenge.

Green Tower route

The Green Tower cluster includes the Orange Barn, number sequence commonly identified by “4166 / 1899,” Blue Signal, Yellow Stand and Beach House challenges. Split observation and interaction roles before pressing anything.

Blue, Yellow & Black Tower routes

Later routes continue the same communication-first design with heavier objects, locked rooms, color tiles, telescopes, timed tasks and pattern houses. We avoid presenting a fixed “all puzzles” count because public naming and documentation continue to evolve.

Tip: Screenshot clues for your own group, but describe the relationship between symbols rather than reading every object aloud. It keeps voice chat useful.
