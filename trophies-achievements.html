Completion guide

Big Walk Trophies & Achievements

The cleanest achievement run is usually a second pass. Let your group discover the main journey first, then use the map and unlocked transport to revisit missed activities.

Quick answer: Do not assume the first ending means every achievement or optional challenge is complete. Keep a separate completion checklist after the group reaches the end of the main route.

Community gameplay screenshot supplied by the site owner. This independent guide is not affiliated with House House or Panic.

Natural progress

  • Opening routes and major world systems provide many early milestones.
  • Train, chairlift and tunnel progression are useful completion anchors.
  • Group-based moments are easier when the regular team is present.

Cleanup strategy

  • Record skipped puzzles and unusual items as you find them.
  • Group remaining tasks by map area instead of running back and forth.
  • Check platform-specific trophy or achievement lists after updates.

Avoiding spoilers

  • Keep ending and post-game details separate from beginner pages.
  • Let players opt into full-solution guides.
  • Mark guides with a last-checked date when a patch may change requirements.
