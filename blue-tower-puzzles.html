Blue Tower Puzzles & Train Route

Blue Tower progression is tied to the railway. Treat the route as a navigation project: collect rewards, prepare the key and learn the stations as a group.

Quick answer: Blue Tower progression is tied to the railway. Treat the route as a navigation project: collect rewards, prepare the key and learn the stations as a group.

What Blue Tower unlocks

The train network makes later exploration and cleanup easier. It becomes far more useful once the group agrees on station names and map landmarks.

Route checklist

Bring the required rewards to the Blue Tower; complete the indicated key route; keep the progression item with one player; use the finished key at the railway destination.

After trains start

Take one complete ride before splitting up; use stations as rendezvous points; pair train stops with nearby skipped puzzles and landmarks.
