Platform guide

Big Walk on PS5

Yes—Big Walk is released on PlayStation 5, supports up to twelve online players and can play across supported platforms.

Quick answer: Big Walk is available for PS5. Online play and PlayStation Plus are required.

PS5 release status

The PS5 edition is available now. Check the PlayStation Store in your region for current price, subscription offers and download details.

Online players

Sessions support 2–12 people. The host selects a 2-player, 3-player or 4+ world configuration based on the reliable core group.

Does PS5 have crossplay?

Yes. PS5 friends can play with supported Steam, Mac and Nintendo Switch 2 players. See the crossplay setup guide.

Voice chat tip

If game voice chat is muted, open the PS5 control center, review Sound settings and make sure “Mute Game Voice Chat” is disabled.

Before you buy

Big Walk has no full solo mode or public matchmaking. Organize at least one other player and remember that PlayStation Plus is required for online play.
