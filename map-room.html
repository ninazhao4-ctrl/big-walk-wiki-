Map Room Guide

The Map Room is an early navigation milestone. Unlocking it gives your group a better way to record landmarks, coordinate clues and later transport routes.

Quick answer: The Map Room is an early navigation milestone. Unlocking it gives your group a better way to record landmarks, coordinate clues and later transport routes.

How the Map Room fits the route

Progress through the Drawbridge and Red Tower route before expecting the map to be available. Treat the map as a reward for early cooperation, not a starting menu.

Group workflow

Let one player read while another checks landmarks in the field; write down coordinate pairs exactly; mark completed towers and transport systems immediately.

Use it for cleanup

Return when a puzzle clue gives a number pair; group unfinished tasks by area; plan train, chairlift and tunnel routes before splitting the team.
