Big Walk Release Date & Price

Big Walk released on August 4, 2026. Store price, discounts and availability can vary by platform and region, so always confirm the final amount on the official storefront.

Release date

  • Big Walk launched on August 4, 2026.
  • It is a cooperative adventure by House House, published by Panic.
  • The game is designed for a regular group of friends rather than random matchmaking.

Where to buy

  • Steam offers the Windows and Mac version.
  • Mac players can also use the Mac App Store.
  • Console versions are available through PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 storefronts.

Before you buy

  • Every player needs access to a supported version to join online.
  • Big Walk supports 2–12 players and crossplay between supported platforms.
  • Check your local storefront for the final price, discounts and regional taxes.
