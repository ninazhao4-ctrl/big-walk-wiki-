When to start

Complete the main route first. Post-game challenges work best when the regular host save and the players who want to finish them are available.

How to track them

List each discovered purple challenge; record its region and the original mechanic it remixes; mark progress as a group rather than assuming every player has the same memory.

Cleanup advice

Use unlocked transport to reduce backtracking; finish one map region at a time; compare your list with the 100% completion checklist before revisiting the final area.
