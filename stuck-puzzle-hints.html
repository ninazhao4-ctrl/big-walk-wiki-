Stuck Puzzle Hints

Use this page before opening a complete walkthrough. Most Big Walk puzzles ask your group to share information, coordinate timing or bring an object to the right place.

Quick answer: Use this page before opening a complete walkthrough. Most Big Walk puzzles ask your group to share information, coordinate timing or bring an object to the right place.

First hint

Count players, visible objects and places where someone can stand. Ask what one player can see, hear or reach that another cannot.

Second hint

Test whether distance, timing, sound or a change of viewpoint alters the challenge. Do not move every object at once; change one thing, then observe.

When to use a full guide

Open a complete solution when the group agrees it wants spoilers, when accessibility makes repeated experimentation unhelpful, or when you have confirmed a possible bug.
