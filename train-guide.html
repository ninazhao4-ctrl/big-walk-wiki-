Train Guide

Quick answer: Once trains are unlocked, stations become reliable meeting points and a useful way to clean up distant puzzles.

What to do

Take one complete ride together first; name the station and nearby landmark; decide the return station before anyone leaves.

Helpful checks

Trains reduce walking only when the group has a shared map plan, so pair every ride with a specific objective.

Frequently asked questions

Is this official information?

Multiplayer, saves, joining, communication and troubleshooting facts are checked against the official Big Walk FAQ. Route notes are fan-made and should be rechecked after updates.

What if our group is still stuck?

Regroup at a known landmark, confirm the host and session state, then work through one check at a time rather than changing several things at once.
