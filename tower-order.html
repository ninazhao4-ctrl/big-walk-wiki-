Route planning

Best Tower Order

There is room to explore in a different order, but a navigation-first route helps most new groups avoid repeated walking and unclear directions.

Quick answer: There is room to explore in a different order, but a navigation-first route helps most new groups avoid repeated walking and unclear directions.

Suggested order

Red Tower for the map; Blue Tower for trains; Green Tower for chairlift access; Yellow Tower for tunnels. This order favors information before convenience.

When to change course

Follow a nearby tower if your team is already carrying suitable rewards; switch if the current group size fits another challenge better; choose the unlock your regular group is most excited to use.

Keep the route clear

Assign a navigator; keep the key carrier with the main team; record each completed system on the map before moving to the next tower.
