Route planning

Best Big Walk Tower Order

Start with Red, then choose Green, Yellow, and Blue around the travel options your group wants to unlock first.

Quick answer: Build the Red Tower first. A reliable default is Green → Yellow → Blue, but Green, Yellow, and Blue can be swapped when another travel option better fits your group.

Recommended tower order

  1. Red Tower — make this the first tower your group builds.
  2. Green Tower — choose it next when it best supports the route your group wants to take.
  3. Yellow Tower — continue here, or move it earlier if its travel option is more useful now.
  4. Blue Tower — complete it when its travel option is the best fit for your plan.

Why start with Red?

Red is the dependable first milestone for this community route. It gives the team a clear shared start before you decide how to shape the rest of the session. Confirm it together, then decide on the next tower as a group rather than treating every later tower as a fixed requirement.

Green, Yellow, and Blue are flexible

The order after Red is not one mandatory solution. Swap Green, Yellow, and Blue depending on your preferred mode of travel, the area you want to explore next, and which unlocked option will save the most time for the current session. Green → Yellow → Blue is a useful default, not a rule.

Before moving on

  • Make sure everyone knows which tower is next.
  • Choose around your travel plan, not only tower colour.
  • Record completed routes so a later session can continue cleanly.

Tower order FAQ

Do I have to build the towers in one fixed order?

No. Red is the recommended first tower here. Green, Yellow, and Blue can be rearranged to fit the travel option your group prefers.

What is the default route after Red?

Our default community route is Green, then Yellow, then Blue.

Can a group change plans part way through?

Yes. Recheck where the team wants to go next and choose the tower that makes the most sense for that session.
