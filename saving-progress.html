How Saving Progress Works

Big Walk saves progress automatically through the host’s save game.

Quick answer: Big Walk saves progress automatically through the host’s save game.

What to do

Keep the same person as the regular host; ask that player to start each continued session; confirm everyone understands they will disconnect when the host stops.

Helpful checks

If the group changes hosts, treat it as a different session unless the original save host is available.

Frequently asked questions

Is this official information?

Multiplayer, saves, joining, communication and troubleshooting facts are checked against the official Big Walk FAQ. Route notes are fan-made and should be rechecked after updates.

What if our group is still stuck?

Regroup at a known landmark, confirm the host and session state, then work through one check at a time rather than changing several things at once.
