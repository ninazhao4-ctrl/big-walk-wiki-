Platform guide

Big Walk on Steam

The Steam release supports Windows and Mac and connects with friends on other released platforms through crossplay.

Quick info: Big Walk is available on Steam for Windows and Mac. Regional prices, compatibility labels and live player data can change, so check the Steam client before buying.

Big Walk Steam price

Pricing varies by region and promotions. This guide intentionally avoids hard-coding a store price that may become stale; verify the current total in your local Steam store.

Steam Deck support

Check the current compatibility badge on the Steam product page before purchase. Even when a game runs well, voice input, text size and control prompts can affect a cooperative session.

Player count & charts

Live concurrent-player numbers move constantly. Steam’s community hub and third-party chart tools may show estimates, but they are not the same as Big Walk’s 2–12 players-per-session limit.

Steam keys

Use an authorized storefront and activate a legitimate key in the Steam client. This wiki does not sell keys or direct readers to gray-market listings.

Steam crossplay

Steam players can join friends on PS5, Nintendo Switch 2 and other supported releases. Read the full crossplay checklist.

Mac support

Big Walk is available for Mac through Steam and the Mac App Store. Make sure the edition and device meet the current store requirements.
